ClickSafe Content Agent

A personal automation client for producing, quality-checking, uploading, and measuring educational cybersecurity content for the ClickSafe Daily channel.

What the client does

  • Reads the authorized channel's YouTube analytics and basic channel/video metadata.
  • Selects the next topic from an owner-approved content plan.
  • Generates an educational video package and applies fact-check and technical quality gates.
  • Uploads only to the explicitly authorized ClickSafe Daily YouTube channel.
  • Supports owner control over video title, description, audience designation, and visibility.
  • Uses YouTube API Services only after Google OAuth authorization.

Owner control and YouTube actions

YouTube write actions are associated with the authorized ClickSafe Daily channel. The client is configured by its owner with the intended upload cadence, content scope, metadata behavior, and final visibility. Automatic publication remains disabled until the applicable YouTube API compliance and OAuth readiness gates are satisfied.

The owner can stop the automation, change its publication mode, remove stored authorization, or revoke Google access at any time.

Upload certification

Before upload functionality is enabled, the owner must certify that uploaded content complies with the YouTube Terms of Service, including the YouTube Community Guidelines, and must not violate the copyright or privacy rights of others.

Data and privacy
